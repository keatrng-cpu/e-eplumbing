Plan FAQ

Questions about the plan

How much is it?

$29 a month, or $279 a year paid up front (that saves $69).

When do the visits happen?

We schedule them with you — typically the inspection and heater flush in the fall before freeze-up, and the sump test in early spring before the melt.

Does the 10% apply to emergencies?

The 10% (up to $120 per repair) applies to repairs, and every repair is quoted flat-rate before work starts. Ask us about your specific job.

Call or email us and we’ll walk you through it — plan terms are confirmed when you sign up.
