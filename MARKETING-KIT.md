## Social posts (Nextdoor / Facebook)

**Helpful, not salesy — rotate weekly:**

1. Before a hard freeze: “Drip the far bathroom faucet on subzero nights and open cabinet doors on exterior walls. If a line freezes anyway, call before you try a torch.”
2. Before spring melt: “Test your sump this weekend. Pour a bucket in the pit and confirm discharge outside. Weak pump + check-valve issues are cheaper before the river rises.”
3. Hard water: “White crust on faucets isn’t just ugly — it shortens water heater life. Softeners and flushes pay for themselves in this valley.”
4. Emergency: “Burst pipe checklist: main shut-off → power off near water → call a licensed plumber. We answer 24/7 at (218) 779-2553.”
