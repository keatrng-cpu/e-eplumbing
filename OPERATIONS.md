# E&E Plumbing — Operations Playbook

Day-to-day operating system for service delivery, from first ring to review request.

---

## 1. Phone & Form Intake Script

### Opening (phone)
> “E&E Plumbing, this is [Name] — how can I help you?”

### Qualify in 60–90 seconds
1. **Name + callback number** (confirm it)
2. **Town / address** (service area check)
3. **What’s happening?** (listen; don’t interrupt)
4. **Urgency** — water actively flowing / no water / drain backup / just planning?
5. **Access** — locked gate, dogs, basement entry, HOA rules?
6. **Preferred window** if not emergency

### Emergency triage (always offer)
- Shut off main if water is escaping
- Kill power near standing water
- “I’m dispatching now — ETA ___”

### Close
> “I’ll text you a confirmation with the arrival window. Flat-rate quote before we start any work. Sound good?”

Create the job in Jobber / Housecall Pro **before** hanging up or while driving.

### Website form handling
- Netlify Forms → email + SMS (Zapier or Netlify notifications)
- Target response: **< 15 minutes** during office hours, **< 30 minutes** after hours for “Emergency — now”
- Always call back; never only email

---

## 2. The E&E Standard (every job)

Printed on a card in every truck and in the app job notes:

1. Call / text ahead with real arrival window — and hit it
2. Boot covers on, floor runners down, workspace protected
3. Options explained in plain English — no scare tactics
4. Flat-rate quote approved **before** work starts
5. Photos of the finished work in the customer’s inbox
6. Site left cleaner than we found it
7. Review request sent same day or next morning

---

## 3. Flat-Rate Quoting Rules

- Present **2–3 options** when reasonable (good / better / best).
- Include parts + labor + trip in one number.
- After-hours / weekend / holiday multiplier is stated up front.
- If the job scope changes mid-work, **stop and re-quote** before continuing.
- Never “time and materials” for residential service unless the customer explicitly requests it and signs off.

Build the pricebook in the FSM software. Start with the top 30 jobs from the website service list.

---

## 4. Dispatch & Truck Standards

**Minimum stocked truck (Phase 1):**
- Common repair parts (supply stops, cartridges, wax rings, P-traps, etc.)
- Water heater connectors / expansion tank
- Sump pump + check valve
- Basic drain tools + camera if available
- Freeze protection materials in winter
- Boot covers, drop cloths, shop vac, PPE

**Job packet (digital):**
- Customer name, phone, address, gate codes
- Problem description + photos from customer if any
- Prior history in CRM
- Quoted options ready to present

---

## 5. Payment & Invoicing

- Prefer card or ACH same day via FSM app.
- Deposits required on equipment installs (water heaters, softeners) — typically 30–50%.
- Net-15 only for established commercial accounts with credit approval.
- No work leaves the property unpaid without written exception from the owner.

---

## 6. Review & Reputation Loop

1. After job completion → automated text/email with Google review link
2. If 4–5 star language in conversation, ask in person: “Would you mind leaving us a quick Google review? It helps other Valley families find us.”
3. Negative feedback → owner calls within 24 hours; make it right before it becomes a public review when possible
4. Replace the three sample testimonials on the website as soon as real ones exist

---

## 7. Home Team Plan (membership)

**What members get (finalize numbers before public launch):**
- Annual whole-home plumbing inspection
- Water heater flush
- Sump test before melt season
- Priority scheduling
- Member discount on repairs
- Reminder system for seasonal maintenance

Sell at the end of every successful residential job. Track conversion rate.

---

## 8. After-Hours Protocol

- Phone forwarded to on-call licensed plumber
- Same intake script
- Higher rate disclosed on the call
- Job still enters the CRM the same night
- Follow-up office call next business morning for any incomplete paperwork

---

## 9. Safety & Compliance

- Always verify shut-offs and electrical safety before entering wet areas
- Permit jobs that require permits — pull them, meet the inspector
- Keep license numbers, bond, and insurance certificates in the truck and on the website footer
- Document with photos for every significant job (before / during / after)

---

## 10. Weekly Operating Rhythm

| Day | Focus |
|-----|--------|
| Mon | Review weekend emergencies, schedule week, parts order |
| Tue–Thu | Core service days |
| Fri | Catch-up, membership pushes, marketing posts |
| Weekend | On-call only (unless backlog) |

**Owner metrics every Friday:** leads, booked, completed, revenue, outstanding invoices, new reviews, memberships sold.
