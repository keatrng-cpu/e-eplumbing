What we do

Built for Valley plumbing problems

Hard water, spring melt, and minus-thirty Januarys — our region breaks plumbing in its own special ways. We fix it in ours.

24/7

Emergency Repairs

Burst lines, sewer backups, failed shut-offs. A licensed plumber answers the phone — day, night, blizzard.

Drain & Sewer

Camera inspections, augering, and hydro jetting for lines choked by roots, grease, or Valley clay silt.

Water Heaters

Tank and tankless — sized for households that run showers, laundry, and a hockey bag wash cycle at once.

Flood Zone

Sump Pumps

Primary pumps, battery backups, and check-valve service — ready before the Red River says it’s spring.

Winter

Frozen & Burst Pipes

Safe electric thawing, repair, and re-routing of freeze-prone runs so one cold snap never bites twice.

Softeners & Filtration

Valley water is famously hard. Softeners, iron filters, and RO systems that protect fixtures and taste right.

Remodels & Rough-In

Kitchen and bath re-pipes, basement bathroom additions, and fixture upgrades done to code in both states.

Commercial

Service contracts, backflow testing, and build-outs for shops, restaurants, and farm operations.

By the book

Code-first, on both sides of the line

Depending on which side of the river you call home, your job is built to the North Dakota State Plumbing Code (ND State Plumbing Board) or the 2020 Minnesota Plumbing Code, Chapter 4714 — Minnesota’s amended adoption of the 2018 Uniform Plumbing Code. Master Plumber supervision on every project.

Venting & DWV

Drain, waste, and vent systems designed to code — correct venting protects trap seals and keeps sewer gas where it belongs: out of your house.

Sized by the Numbers

Water, gas, and waste lines sized from fixture-unit calculations and code tables — trap sizes included, rules of thumb excluded.

Isometrics & Permits

Remodels and rough-ins mapped in isometric schematics for permit submittal. We pull the permit and meet the inspector — you don’t have to.

Backflow Prevention

Installation and testing of backflow assemblies that keep the drinking-water supply protected — with test records filed for you annually.
