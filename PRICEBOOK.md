# E&E Flat-Rate Pricebook

Starting structure for Jobber / Housecall Pro. **Calibrate every number against local parts cost + your labor rate before using live.**

Ranges below are Midwest residential starting points (2026), not final prices.

**Rules**
- Quote includes trip, labor, standard parts for that line item unless noted.
- After-hours / weekend / holiday: **1.5×** the listed rate (state on the call).
- If scope changes mid-job → stop and re-quote.
- Equipment installs (water heater, softener, sump): collect **30–50% deposit** before ordering.
