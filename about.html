FAQ

What if water is moving at 2 a.m.?

Call (218) 779-2553 — do not wait on the form. Shut off the main water if it is safe, keep power away from standing water, and tell us what you see.

Do you serve Minnesota and North Dakota?

Yes — we serve both sides of the Red River. Availability and any permitting requirements depend on the job and jurisdiction, so call if you are unsure.

Will I know the price before work starts?

Flat-rate from the pricebook, approved first. After-hours carries a published multiplier — still quoted before we start.

Same-day water heater?

Often, when the unit is a common size we stock. Tank and tankless, sized for real Valley households.

How do I pay?

Same-day invoice, card or check.
